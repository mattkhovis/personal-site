Line by Line

Privacy

Last updated 2026-09-21

The short version

Line by Line is a game about reading code. The iPhone and iPad app collects nothing and sends nothing: there is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and your progress stays on your device.

The web version also keeps your progress in your browser. Some of its features use the internet; each is listed below with exactly what it sends and who can see it, and most are off until you turn them on.

In the iPhone and iPad app

Your save (your badge name, your progress and your answer history) is kept on your device, in the app's own storage. The app has no network code, so it cannot send it anywhere.

Things leave the device only when you send them:

  • Exporting a save writes a file you choose where to put.
  • Sharing a run code or a Daily Read result goes through the system share sheet, to whoever you pick. A run code can carry your badge name and your best time, never your answers.
  • Importing reads a save file or a run code you chose to open. A run code from a friend can show their badge name.
  • On the United States App Store, the title screen has a Support the game on Ko-fi link. It opens ko-fi.com in Safari; the app sends nothing with it, and what happens there is covered by Ko-fi's own privacy policy.

The app's privacy manifest, which Apple reads, declares that it collects nothing and tracks nothing.

In the web version

Your saves and settings are kept in your browser's own storage. Clearing your browser's data for the site removes them.

Loading the page fetches its fonts from Google Fonts and its code highlighter from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare). As with any web request, those servers receive your IP address and browser details; the game sends them nothing else.

The web version is published on claude.ai, which runs it and stores anything the features below save. That storage is Anthropic's, under Anthropic's privacy policy. The game itself has no server.

Web features that use the internet

None of these exist in the app. On claude.ai:

  • Cloud sync: when you are signed in to claude.ai, your save slots are stored under your account so you can carry on from another device. Your settings join them only if you turn on Sync settings across devices. Deleting a slot deletes it there too.
  • Share anonymous answer stats: off until you turn it on. Sends how often each question is answered, answered right first time, timed out, and which wrong answer was picked, under a random id that is not your account. Used to find questions that are unclear or wrong.
  • Send error reports: off until you turn it on. Sends a game error's message and where in the code it happened, with the game's version and the screen you were on. Web addresses and anything that looks like an email address are removed first.
  • Report a problem with a question: only when you send one. Sends the question, the reason you picked and your note, if you write one. Not your name or account. Anyone who can open the game could read reports, so keep personal details out of the note.
  • Daily Read board: only when you press Post to today's board. Shows your badge name, score and time to everyone who opens the board. You can delete your entry.
  • Teams: only if you join one. The team sees your badge name and a progress summary: floors cleared, stars, streak, and which floors you finished and when. Never your answers. Sharing your per-skill totals, or your Explain it back answers with the team owner, are separate switches, off until you turn them on. Notes and packs you send to the team are seen by the team.
  • Live duels and class quizzes: while you are in one, the other players see your badge name and progress, and a quiz host sees each answer you give. Nothing is kept once it ends.
  • Ask Mags, Explain it back grading, Make a pack from my code and Review a real PR send the question and what you typed or pasted to Claude through your own claude.ai account, so they fall under your account's terms, not the game's. The game does not keep the conversation. Before a paste is sent, the game looks for keys, tokens and passwords and asks you to remove them.
  • Discuss this question: opens claude.ai's own comments. What you post there is posted by claude.ai under your account.
  • Dictation: uses your browser's speech recognition, which some browsers run on an online service of their own.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included. The web features that store or send anything run on claude.ai and need a claude.ai account, which is subject to claude.ai's own terms, including its age requirements.

Your choices

  • Every feature above that is off by default has its switch in Settings or on the screen it belongs to, and turning it off stops it.
  • Cloud sync has no switch: it runs whenever you play on claude.ai while signed in. Playing signed out keeps everything in your browser.
  • In the app, deleting the app deletes your save. On the web, Saves can delete any slot, and clearing the site's data removes everything the browser kept.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Changes

If the game starts collecting or sending something new, this page changes before the game does, and the date at the top changes with it.
