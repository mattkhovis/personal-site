Line by Line

Support

Line by Line is a game about reading code, for iPhone, iPad and the web. There's no account and nothing to sign in to, so most problems are quick to sort out by email.

Get help

Email matt.hovis@icloud.com with what happened, what device you're on, and how far you'd got. Bug reports, questions about a specific question or explanation, and anything else about the game are all welcome here.

Common questions

  • Is my progress backed up anywhere? Your save lives on your device only. In the app, use Settings > Saves to export a copy as a file, or as a short code you can paste back in later.
  • Can I move a save between the app and the web version? Yes — a save exported from one opens in the other. Settings > Saves has both Export and Import.
  • Does the app collect any data? No. See the privacy policy for the full answer.
