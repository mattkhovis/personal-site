Shiplog

Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-16

The short version

Shiplog is a journal for software developers: it reads activity from GitHub and keeps it, with your own notes, on your device. Your log stays on your device. There is no account, no server of Shiplog's own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in Shiplog is sent to its developer.

What Shiplog keeps, and where

  • Your log: the repositories you follow, the GitHub activity Shiplog has read for them, and everything you write (notes, courses, stars, tags, whales and their harpoons) is saved in Shiplog's own storage on your device.
  • Photos you attach to notes stay on the device where you attached them. They aren't sent anywhere, and leave the device only inside a backup file you make and share yourself.
  • Your GitHub token, if you add one, is kept in your device's Keychain, readable only by Shiplog on that device, and sent only to GitHub with Shiplog's own requests. Remove it at any time in the Harbour.
  • Your settings (reading preferences, which tips you've seen and the like) are saved on your device.

Who Shiplog talks to

  • GitHub. To fill your log, Shiplog asks GitHub's API (api.github.com) about the repositories you name: their commits, pull requests, workflow runs, issues, and the GitHub account names behind them. The requests go straight from your device to GitHub, with your token if you've added one. GitHub's privacy statement covers what GitHub does with them.

Shiplog sends nothing to anyone else. It contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and its privacy manifest, which Apple reads, declares that it collects nothing and tracks nothing.

Notifications, widgets, Siri and Spotlight

The evening reminder and CI alerts are local notifications, scheduled and shown by your device. If you allow it, Shiplog checks GitHub in the background so they stay current. Widgets and Spotlight show your log from data kept on your device, and Siri and Shortcuts actions run in Shiplog on your device.

Your choices

  • Remove your GitHub token: the Harbour, under GitHub lookout.
  • Remove a repository and everything logged for it: when you follow more than one, press and hold it in the Harbour's fleet and choose Strike from the fleet.
  • Clear everything: the Harbour, Start a fresh sample voyage.
  • Remove everything from a device: delete the app, and its log and photos go with it. iOS may keep the Keychain token until you remove it in the Harbour first.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Children

Shiplog isn't directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If Shiplog starts keeping or sending something new, this page changes before the app does, and the date at the top changes with it.
