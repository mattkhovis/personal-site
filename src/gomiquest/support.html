GomiQuest

Support

GomiQuest is a litter-collecting game for iPhone: log what you pick up, trace your route and watch your impact add up. There's no account and nothing to sign in to, so most problems are quick to sort out by email.

Get help

Email matt.hovis@icloud.com with what happened, what iPhone and iOS version you're on, and what you were doing. Bug reports, questions and ideas are all welcome.

Common questions

  • Is it really free? Yes. GomiQuest has no ads, no in-app purchases, no microtransactions and no subscriptions, and everything is included from the start. On the United States App Store, Profile has an optional link to tip the developer on Ko-fi; it unlocks nothing.
  • Where is my data? On your phone only. GomiQuest has no account and no server. Read the privacy policy for the full answer.
  • How do I move to a new phone? Restoring a phone from an iPhone backup brings your history with it. You can also use Profile > Export my data to save a backup file and Profile > Import a backup on the new phone.
  • How do I delete everything? Profile > Delete all my data removes your items, sessions, photos and settings from the phone. Workouts you chose to save to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you remove them there.
  • Why does it ask for location? To pin what you collect and to trace your route while a cleanup session is running, including with the screen off. It never tracks you outside a session. The optional "arrive at an adopted spot" reminder is off until you turn it on.
  • Is it safe to pick up needles or glass? Never use bare hands. GomiQuest only logs hazardous items through its safety steps, and children should go with an adult.
