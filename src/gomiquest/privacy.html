GomiQuest

Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-01

The short version

GomiQuest is a litter-collecting game. It collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else: there is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code, and everything you log stays on your iPhone.

What the app keeps on your phone

The items you log, your cleanup sessions (times, distances and routes), notes, photos you attach, your display name, your settings and your progress are stored in the app's own storage on your device. The app has no network code of its own, so it cannot send any of this anywhere.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (while using the app). Pins what you collect and traces your route during a cleanup session, including while the screen is off. iOS shows its location indicator while this is happening. The route is stored on your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Location (always) – optional. Off unless you turn on arrival reminders for a spot you adopted. It watches only those places and does not record your movements.
  • Apple Health – optional, write only. If you turn on "Save sessions to Health", a finished session is saved as a walking workout with distance and route. GomiQuest never reads your health data.
  • Calendar – write only. Adds an event when you ask. It cannot read your calendar.
  • Notifications. Reminders you choose are scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server.
  • Photos. You pick a photo with the system picker; GomiQuest sees only the photo you choose.

What leaves your phone, and only by your choice

  • Exports and shares. A backup or export file, a share card or a diagnostics report is created only when you ask, and goes wherever you send it from the iOS share sheet. Exports hide where your routes start and end unless you turn that off, and a diagnostics report contains no logged items, locations or photos.
  • Ko-fi link (United States App Store only). Profile may show an optional link to tip the developer. It opens ko-fi.com in Safari; the app sends nothing with it, and Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies there. It unlocks nothing.

What Apple handles

  • Maps. The map is Apple Maps, so your phone requests map tiles from Apple under Apple's terms.
  • Backups. Your phone's iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data, as it does for most apps, encrypted under your Apple Account's settings. Temporary recovery copies and diagnostics files are excluded.
  • Health, widgets and Live Activities. Workouts you save are in Apple Health under Apple's terms. Widgets and Live Activities are drawn by iOS from data already on your phone.

Deleting your data

Profile > Delete all my data removes everything GomiQuest holds from the phone: items, sessions, photos, settings, reminders and recovery copies. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you remove them in the Health app, and older phone backups keep their copy until they are replaced.

Children

GomiQuest is meant for adults, and children should go with one. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either.
